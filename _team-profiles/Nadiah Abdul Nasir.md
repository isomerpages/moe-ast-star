<p>A reflective and dedicated educator with over 15 years of experience in
Visual Arts and Aesthetics Education, committed to nurturing students'
creativity, critical thinking and artistic confidence through meaningful
and authentic learning experiences.</p>
<p>Her leadership journey began at Regent Secondary School, where she served
as Subject Head (Art), fostering a culture of creativity and inquiry. These
experiences laid a strong foundation for her subsequent role as Subject
Head (Aesthetics) at Bukit Batok Secondary School, where she provided strategic
leadership in advancing Aesthetics Education, driving curriculum innovation
and creating meaningful opportunities for students to engage with the arts
across the school.</p>
<p>Beyond her school, she actively contributes to the professional learning
of educators. Her commitment to educational leadership was strengthened
through her participation in the Management and Leadership in Schools (MLS)
Programme in 2023, which deepened her understanding of strategic leadership,
organisational development and leading sustainable school improvement.
Building on these experiences, she serves as Co-Lead of the W7 Aesthetics
Networked Learning Community (NLC) for Aesthetics, working alongside Art
and Music educators across the primary and secondary levels to reimagine
assessment practices that strengthen student agency and enhance the teaching
and learning of Arts Education throughout the W7 cluster. Through this
professional learning community, she works alongside fellow educators to
foster collaborative inquiry, share positive and inclusive classroom practices
and support the continuous improvement of Arts Education.</p>
<p>Grounded in collaboration, continuous learning and service, she is committed
to empowering both students and educators while building inclusive learning
communities where creativity, innovation and meaningful partnerships enable
every individual to flourish.</p>
